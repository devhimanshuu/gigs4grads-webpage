Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure

Engineered for trust, safety, and rapid execution

Gigs4Grads removes the friction from hiring and freelancing with integrated KYB checks, geofencing, escrow, and structured readiness.

Verified KYB & ID Checks

Zero anonymous actors. Companies submit verified legal registration and EIN. Talent completes biometric ID checks before entering bidding.

Dynamic Fit Scoring

A 5-factor weighted algorithm evaluates skills (30%), venue distance (20%), availability (20%), experience (15%), and reputation tier (15%).

Geofenced GPS Check-In

Eliminate no-shows. Attendance tracking uses 200m geofence radii and unique venue QR codes for timestamped check-in and check-out confirmation.

Automated Escrow Security

Gig payments are authorized and escrowed in advance. Funds release automatically upon completed report submission and employer review.

Briefings & Readiness Quizzes

Ensure talent is prepared before arriving. Companies attach talking points and interactive quizzes that talent must pass before being marked Ready.

Portable Reputation Tiers

Early career graduates advance from Entry → Verified → Pro tiers. Higher tier talent enjoys lower platform fees and priority access to premium gigs.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about eligibility, payments, and platform security.

Current undergraduate students, graduate students, and recent university graduates residing in the United States with valid U.S. work authorization are eligible. All talent completes identity verification prior to submitting their first gig bid.
When a company selects a candidate for a gig, the agreed compensation is authorized and locked into escrow immediately. Talent knows the money is guaranteed, and companies have reassurance that funds only release once the venue report and check-out are verified.
The mobile app confirms attendance via a 200-meter GPS geofence around the event venue or by scanning the manager's check-in QR code. The app never tracks location in the background; verification occurs only upon arrival and departure.
Talent progresses across Entry (20% fee), Verified (17% fee), and Pro (15% fee) tiers. Advancing requires completed gigs with high ratings, on-time check-ins, and verified reliability. Pro tier members receive priority matching for premium assignments.
